Frequently asked

Do you deliver and install outside Nashik?

Nashik and nearby areas are covered by our own delivery and installation team. For other places, message us on WhatsApp with your location and we'll tell you what's possible and what transport will cost.

How do I get a quotation?

Every custom piece is different, so we don't quote over chat. Book a call back or a workshop visit; we'll understand your requirement, visit your site if needed, and then give you a written quotation.

How long does a custom order take?

It depends on the size, the material and how much turning or carving is involved. We write the delivery date into your quotation before we start. In the shop, every made-to-order product shows its lead time.

Can I visit the workshop?

Yes, you're welcome any day between 10 AM and 7 PM. Book a workshop visit so the right person is free to show you samples and finished work.

How does payment work?

Payment terms are written in your quotation before any work starts, so you know exactly what is due and when. Shop orders are confirmed with you on WhatsApp.

Do you work with architects and interior designers?

Yes. We regularly build to architects' and designers' drawings, and we're happy to credit them on our completed projects.

Can you match an existing piece, drawing or photo?

Yes. Send a drawing, a photo or a sample piece. Our lathe department can match a turned profile, and our carpenters can match sizes and details of existing furniture.

Materials

Woods we work with

Every solid wood piece is made from seasoned timber, so it doesn't warp or crack in Nashik's dry summers and wet monsoons.

Teak

Tectona grandis

Oily, stable, termite resistant. Our first choice for doors, mandirs and heirloom pieces.

Sheesham

Dalbergia sissoo

Dense Indian rosewood with bold grain. Beds, dining sets and sofas.

Mango

Mangifera indica

Lighter and more affordable. Good for side tables and small pieces.

Rubberwood

Hevea brasiliensis

Even, pale grain that takes stain well. Chairs and budget dining sets.

Care guide

Looking after solid wood

Solid wood lasts generations if it's kept dry and dusted. A few habits make the difference.

Everyday cleaning

Dust with a dry, soft cotton cloth. For marks, use a cloth that is only slightly damp and dry the surface straight away. Avoid soaking wet cloths, harsh cleaners and spray polishes with silicone.

Sun, AC and moisture

Keep pieces out of long, direct sunlight and away from AC vents and wet walls. Wood moves with humidity, so steady conditions keep joints tight and polish even.

Heat and spills

Use coasters and mats under hot vessels and glasses. Wipe spills immediately, especially water, oil and turmeric.

Termites and pests

Teak is naturally resistant, and all our wood is seasoned. Keep furniture a little away from damp walls and floors, and let us know at once if you see powder or small holes.

Polishing and touch-ups

Polished surfaces can be refreshed every few years. Ask us when your piece starts looking dull or scratched and we'll advise whether it needs a touch-up or a full re-polish.

About Us

A Nashik workshop, run by the same family since 1991.

Amar Furniture began in 1991 as a small solid wood workshop in Nashik. Over 35 years it has grown into three departments: wooden furniture, modular kitchens and home furniture, and a lathe department for turned work.

Everything is made in-house: design, cutting, joinery, turning, polishing and installation. That is why we can make a mandir that matches your door frames, or a railing whose balusters are identical from the first to the last.

We work directly with homeowners, and alongside architects, interior designers and builders across Nashik.
